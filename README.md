# Used-Cars-price-prediction
What drives the price of a car?

## Overview

The goal of this project is to understand what factors influence the price of used cars. The analysis uses a dataset of used vehicle listings and follows the CRISP-DM framework to clean the data, explore important relationships, build regression models, and provide recommendations to a used car dealership.

## Key Findings

- Newer vehicles are generally associated with higher prices.
- Higher mileage is associated with lower vehicle prices.
- Vehicle condition has an important relationship with price. New vehicles tend to have higher prices, while salvage and fair-condition vehicles tend to have lower prices.
- Manufacturer is strongly associated with vehicle price, with several luxury and performance manufacturers showing higher prices.
- Diesel fuel and 4WD were also associated with higher predicted prices.
- Linear Regression, Ridge Regression, and Lasso Regression produced very similar results.
- The models had an average prediction error of approximately $5,600.

## Recommendations

Used car dealers should consider vehicle year, mileage, condition, manufacturer, fuel type, drivetrain, and vehicle type when purchasing and pricing inventory. Newer, lower-mileage vehicles in better condition may provide stronger inventory opportunities. Dealers should be more cautious when purchasing high-mileage or salvage vehicles because these characteristics are associated with lower prices.
